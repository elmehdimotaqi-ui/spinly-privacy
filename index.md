# Spinly — Privacy Policy

**Effective date:** 27 September 2026

Spinly is a decision spinner. This policy explains what happens to data when you use it.

## What Spinly stores

Your lists, your options, and your spin history are stored **only on your device**, in the app's
own storage. They are never uploaded, never synced, and never sent to us. There is no account, no
login, and no server belonging to Spinly.

Deleting the app deletes all of it.

Spinly also stores small preferences on your device: sound and haptics toggles, spin duration,
how many times the app has been launched (used to decide when to ask for an App Store review),
and whether you have bought Remove Ads.

## What the ads collect

The free version of Spinly shows ads through **Google AdMob**. AdMob is operated by Google, and
when it serves an ad it may collect:

- **Device identifiers**, including the advertising identifier
- **Advertising data**, such as which ads were shown and interacted with
- **Product interaction data**, such as app opens and ad views
- **Approximate location** derived from the network connection, and diagnostic data

This data is collected by Google, not by us — we never receive it. Google's handling of it is
described in the [Google Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy) and in
[How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services](https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites).

## Tracking and your choice

After your first spin, Spinly asks for permission to track your activity across other companies'
apps and websites. This is the standard iOS App Tracking Transparency prompt.

- **If you allow it**, ads may be personalized.
- **If you decline**, or never answer, ads are requested as **non-personalized**. Spinly works
  exactly the same either way — nothing about spinning, your lists, or your history changes.

You can change your answer at any time in **Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking** on your
device.

## Removing ads

Buying **Remove Ads** is a one-time in-app purchase. When it is active, no ads are requested and
AdMob collects nothing. The purchase is handled by Apple; we never see your payment details. Your
entitlement is checked with Apple's StoreKit and is tied to your Apple Account, so you can restore
it on your other devices.

## Children

Spinly is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect personal information
from them.

## Changes

If this policy changes, the effective date above changes with it, and the updated policy is
published at the same address.
