# Spinly — Support

Spinly is a decision spinner for iPhone: make a list, spin the wheel, get an answer.

## Get in touch

**elmehdimotaqi@gmail.com**

Every email is read. If you are reporting something that went wrong, these three things make it
much faster to fix:

1. Your iPhone model and iOS version (Settings → General → About)
2. What you tapped, and what happened instead of what you expected
3. Whether it happens every time, or only sometimes

## Common questions

### I bought Remove Ads and it is not active

Open **Settings → Restore Purchases** inside Spinly. Remove Ads is a one-time purchase tied to the
Apple Account that bought it, so restoring works on any device signed in to that account —
including after deleting and reinstalling the app. Restoring is free and can be done as often as
you like.

If restoring says nothing was found, check that the device is signed in to the same Apple Account
used for the purchase. Apple's purchase history is the record of it: **iOS Settings → your name →
Media & Purchases → Purchase History**.

### My lists disappeared after reinstalling

Spinly keeps your lists and history **on your device only** — nothing is uploaded to a server,
because Spinly has no server and no account. That is good for your privacy, but it means deleting
the app deletes its data, and reinstalling starts fresh. There is no way to recover lists from a
deleted install.

### There is no sound

Two things silence Spinly, and either one is enough:

- the iPhone's **silent switch** — sound follows it by design
- the **Sound** toggle in Spinly's Settings

Haptics are separate and follow your system haptics setting, so a phone with system haptics off
will spin silently and without ticks even when Spinly's own toggle is on.

### The spin is too long, or too short

**Settings → Spin duration** offers Short, Normal and Long.

### How do I clear my spin history?

**Settings → History → Clear all history.** It cannot be undone.

### Can I change whether ads are personalised?

Yes, at any time, in **iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking**. Spinly works identically
either way — declining only means ads are requested as non-personalised. There is no feature
behind that prompt.

### What languages does Spinly speak?

English, French and Arabic, following your device language. Arabic mirrors the whole layout
right-to-left.

## Privacy

Spinly's [privacy policy](https://elmehdimotaqi-ui.github.io/spinly-privacy/) explains exactly what is stored and what the ads collect. The short
version: your lists never leave your device, and buying Remove Ads stops ad requests entirely.
