# Spinly for Android: Privacy Policy

*Google Play listing: "Spinly: Spin the Wheel Picker" (package `com.elmehdi.spinthewheel`).
Effective date: 11 October 2026.*

This page explains what the Android app does with information. In short: **your wheels and history stay on your
phone or tablet. Spinly has no account, no sign-in and no server. Ads come from Google AdMob, and you choose how they
may use your data where the law gives you that choice.**

### 1. What stays on your device

Spinly stores the following **only on your device**, in the app's private storage:

- your wheels (names, emoji, options, weights and colours) and the history of results;
- your settings: sound, haptics, spin duration, appearance (light, dark or system), language, "remove winner by
  default", the selected wheel theme, sound pack, confetti style and pointer;
- which optional extras you have unlocked, and until when the 24-hour ad-free period runs;
- a launch counter, used only to decide when to show Google Play's rating prompt;
- if you choose a custom centre image, a reference to that one picture you picked with the Android photo picker. The
  picture itself is never uploaded.

The developer never receives any of this. Spinly has no analytics of its own. Deleting a wheel or clearing the history
in the app removes it. Uninstalling the app (or Settings → Apps → Spinly → Storage → Clear storage) removes everything.

### 2. Android backup

If you have turned on backup for your device, Android may include Spinly's wheels, history and settings in **your
device backup in your Google Account**, and may copy them when you move to a new phone. Google handles this backup under
your account. The developer can't see or access it. You can turn backup off in your device's settings (usually Settings →
Google → Backup; the path depends on your device).

### 3. Ads (Google AdMob)

Spinly is supported by ads from **Google AdMob** (Google Mobile Ads SDK): a banner, an occasional full-screen ad after a
result, and **rewarded videos you choose to watch**. To serve ads, measure them and prevent fraud, **Google** may collect
and process:

- device identifiers, including the **Android advertising ID**;
- your IP address and the **approximate location** derived from it (Spinly never asks for your location);
- information about your device and the app (model, OS version, language, app version);
- ad interactions, such as which ads were shown or tapped and whether a rewarded video was completed;
- diagnostic data from the ads SDK, such as crash and performance information.

Google collects this data, not the developer. The developer only sees aggregated reports (for example, how many ads were
shown) and never receives information about you. Google's use of this information is described in the
[Google Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy) and in
[How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services](https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites).
Ad traffic is encrypted in transit.

### 4. Your choices about ads

- **Consent (EEA, UK, Switzerland and other places where it is required).** Before any ad is requested, Spinly shows a
  consent message from Google's User Messaging Platform. You can accept, refuse, or choose purposes and partners. If you
  refuse, Google may still show non-personalised or limited ads, which use less data. The app works the same either way.
- **US state privacy laws.** Where they apply, the same message lets you opt out of the "sale" or "sharing" of your
  personal information for targeted advertising.
- **Change your choice at any time:** open Spinly → **Settings → Privacy options**. The row appears whenever your region
  requires a consent choice. Tapping it shows the consent message again, where you can withdraw or change your consent.
- **Advertising ID:** on your device, go to **Settings → Privacy → Ads** (on some devices **Settings → Google → Ads**). You
  can **reset** or **delete** your advertising ID there. After deletion, apps receive a blank ID and ads can't be
  personalised with it.
- **Fewer ads:** watching one rewarded video in Spinly's Settings turns off banners and full-screen ads for 24 hours.
  During that time Spinly doesn't request ads at all, except a rewarded video if you ask for one.

### 5. Rewarded videos and unlocks

Some optional extras (wheel themes, sound packs, confetti styles, pointers, a custom centre image and extra saved-wheel
slots) unlock **for good** when you watch a short rewarded video. One rewarded video also gives 24 hours without ads.
These rewards are recorded **only on your device** (and in your Android backup, if enabled). **Spinly sells nothing:**
there are no in-app purchases or subscriptions, and the app never asks for payment details.

### 6. Permissions

Spinly asks for no runtime permissions: Android never shows you a permission prompt for it. Its manifest declares:

- **Internet** and **network state**, to load ads and open this policy;
- **Vibrate**, for haptic feedback while the wheel spins;
- **Advertising ID** (`AD_ID`) and Android's **ad services** permissions (`ACCESS_ADSERVICES_AD_ID`,
  `ACCESS_ADSERVICES_ATTRIBUTION`, `ACCESS_ADSERVICES_TOPICS`), added by the Google Mobile Ads SDK so it can use Android's
  advertising and Privacy Sandbox APIs;
- **Wake lock** and **foreground service**, added by libraries the ads SDK relies on (Android's WorkManager and Google's
  ad measurement library) to finish short background tasks. Spinly itself runs no background or foreground service.

Spinly doesn't access your location, contacts, camera, microphone, files or photos. The optional centre image uses the
Android photo picker, which shares only the one picture you choose.

### 7. Sharing a result

"Share result" opens Android's share sheet with a short text such as "Spinly picked: Pizza". You choose the app it goes
to, and that app's own policy then applies. Spinly doesn't send it anywhere itself.

### 8. Rating prompt

After you have used the app a few times, Spinly may ask Google Play to show its in-app rating prompt, at most once. Google
Play runs that prompt. Spinly doesn't learn whether you rated it or what you wrote.

### 9. Children

Spinly is not directed at children under 13, and the developer does not knowingly collect personal information from
children. If you believe a child has provided personal information through the app, contact us at the address below.

### 10. Keeping and deleting data

The developer holds no data about you, so there is nothing to delete on the developer's side. To remove what is on your
device, delete wheels or clear history in the app, or uninstall it. For data Google processes for ads, use the choices
in section 4 and Google's own controls at [myadcenter.google.com](https://myadcenter.google.com).
